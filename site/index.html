The local Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture for general spin groups, in Lean

A Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization of the dissertation of Samantha Pease (Rutgers University–Newark, 2026, doi:10.7282/t3-qb3s-s249).

The main theorem is stated and its proof assembled in Lean, but it still rests on unproved steps and on results from the literature assumed as axioms. The blueprint shows which.
